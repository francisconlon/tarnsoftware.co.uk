About the game

You start as a hole barely big enough for a sprinkle. Every donut, macaron and croissant you swallow makes you bigger, until the cupcakes, the tarts and finally the tiered cake drop in too.

  • Tables laid out with care: rows of macarons, towers of donuts, and boards where the treats form a picture.
  • Big treats only fit once you have grown into them, so plan your route.
  • Stacks tip toward the hole as you reach them and tumble in.
  • Some levels give you a shopping list, some a number of treats, some want the whole table cleared.
  • Some boards have no timer at all.
  • Draw your own board on a 12 by 12 grid, then eat it.

Hole Crunch is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. A one-time purchase removes the ads between levels.
